About us

We connect the people making government work better.

We map who is working on state capacity, bring them together to build tools for government, and share what we learn.

Our mission

Connect the people working to improve government. Help them build tools government can use.

Our vision

A government with the technology and talent to deliver for the people it serves.

What we don't do

We don't do government's work for it. We believe there is a time and a place for partnership, but our purpose is to find and connect people and organizations that build government's capacity.
