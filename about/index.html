State Capacity Ecosystem / About Us

About

We make the people working to help government deliver visible to each other, put them in the same room, and turn what they build into something government can use.

Our mission

Map the ecosystem. Put technologists and practitioners in the same room and turn what they build into something government uses. Expand the community. Repeat.

Our vision

A visible, connected ecosystem enabling a government with the talent, tools, and operating capacity to deliver for the people it serves.

What we run
EcosystemWho does what, in one place.
EventsWhere proof gets made.
CommunityWhat we learn, anyone can use and build on.
What we don't do

We don't do government's work for it. We believe there is a time and a place for partnership, but our purpose is to find and connect people and organizations that build government's capacity.
