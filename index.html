A community for better government

We connect the people making government work better.

We're a community of government practitioners, technologists, researchers, funders and everyone else working to help government deliver.

We map the ecosystem of 300+ organizations helping government grow its capacity to deliver.

We host events such as build nights, demo nights, salons and meet-ups, where government practitioners and technologists build tools for government together.

We're building a community that makes this work more visible and keeps the conversation going, through essays, free playbooks and a Slack for people doing the work.

The problem

Good policy fails when the people fixing government work in silos.

State capacity is government's ability to do what it sets out to do: issue the permit, deliver the benefit, keep the trains running.

Plenty of people want to contribute to a government that delivers, but few know who else is out there or how to help. When the work is visible, people find each other and it moves faster.

Inspired by Jennifer Pahlka, Recoding America.

Our mission

Connect the people working to improve government. Help them build tools government can use.

Our vision

A government with the technology and talent to deliver for the people it serves.
