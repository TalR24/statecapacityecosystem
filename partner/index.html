What makes a good problem for a build night
  • It comes up oftenThe task repeats weekly or monthly, or many staff do it.
  • The data existsYou can name the data it needs and share a sample.
  • The output is concreteA table, a map, a summary or a draft.
  • A first version helpsA prototype that is 70% right can still prove the idea.
  • The impact is measurableHours saved, errors caught or people served.

TOO BROAD

"Use data to improve services."

BUILDABLE

"Combine service requests and inspection records into one map that flags locations with repeat requests."

Questions to start with
  • Where does your team spend time pulling information together by hand?
  • What question comes up often that takes too long to answer?
  • What data do you have that you wish you used more?
  • What bigger problem might data or tools help with that you haven't had the time or budget to explore?

A build night is a low-cost place to test it.

What a partnership could involve from your side

One or two problem owners

Staff who know the work and can answer builders' questions.

A pointer to the data

Open data or extracts your agency is comfortable sharing.

A data partner

Your in-house data or performance team would be a natural home for what gets built.

A decision-maker in the room

Someone who can say what's worth taking further.

Judges

Agency staff to review prototypes and give feedback.

A venue

An agency space to host the build night.

Where this could go
  1. Prep sessionOne working session to shape problem statements and identify the data.
  2. Build nightTechnologists and agency practitioners build prototypes together in one evening.
  3. Demo nightA possible follow-on where teams show what they built.
  4. Expert connectionsPractitioners in our network, alongside your agency's own experts.
  5. Continued buildingWe can connect teams that want to keep building with the right people.
Common questions
What does it cost?

Nothing for the agency. Sponsors cover food and space.

Who owns what gets built?

The builders. If your agency wants to take a tool further we can work that out together.

What data would you need?

Only open data or extracts your agency is comfortable sharing. Nothing personal or sensitive.

How many people attend?

Our recent events have drawn 80 to 125+ technologists and practitioners.

Can agency staff take part or judge?

Yes. Staff can join as builders, judges or problem owners.

How much staff time does it take?

A prep conversation, data pointers and the evening itself.

Who handles communications?

We are happy to own communications or follow your agency's lead.

Do staff need technical skills?

No. The strongest teams are led by people who know the problem well.

Have an agency, a venue or a problem in mind?

Tell us and we will scope a build night with you.
