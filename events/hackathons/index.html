Hackathons

Public servants and technologists build working tools in one evening.

We've run two build nights: 80+ builders in June 2026 and about 80 in September 2026. Open one to see what got built.

Past hackathons
September 2026 · NYC
State Capacity Hackathon: Public Engagement
About 80 people at the CUNY Graduate Center built against two scoped themes: tools that make community boards run better and tools that let government hear from residents at scale. Budget Requests won, with Hearts & Minds and In the Zone as runners-up, and all three were invited to the CUNY PIT Lab pop-up at the Oculus.

June 2026 · NYC
A Civic Tech Build Night
Teams used AI agents to build tools for government service delivery in one evening. 80+ people split into 20 teams, and government practitioners, researchers and advocates built alongside the technologists to finish 20 working tools in under two hours.
