How problems are ranked

A problem is a group of voices that share what broke, where in the journey, and who owns it. It must be confirmed by 2+ independent sources and 50+ people before it's scored.

Never counts: views, likes, shares, or how angry a post sounds. Clips tell the story, but they don't set the rank.
