Sponsor benefits

Lead Sponsor Benefits

  • Logo and org credit on event page, image, and description
  • Named in "Hosted By" line
  • Kickoff statement (2–3 min)
  • Closing statement / winner announcement
  • Social amplification credit
  • Early visibility into problem-statement pipeline and resulting prototypes
  • Option to host at own space

Cosponsor Benefits

  • Logo and org credit on event page, image, and description
  • Named in "Hosted By" line
  • Social amplification credit
