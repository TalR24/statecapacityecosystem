Sponsor benefits

Lead Sponsor Benefits

  • Logo and organization credit on the event page, image and description
  • Named in "Hosted By" line
  • Kickoff statement (2–3 min)
  • Closing statement / winner announcement
  • Social amplification credit
  • An early look at the problem statements and the prototypes built from them
  • Option to host at own space

Cosponsor Benefits

  • Logo and organization credit on the event page, image and description
  • Named in "Hosted By" line
  • Social amplification credit

Ready to sponsor a hackathon? Reach out at statecapacityecosystem@gmail.com and we will scope the event with you. Our first build night drew 80+ builders, who made 20 working tools in one evening: see A Civic Tech Build Night.
